Corporate Travel Policy
Version 3.0 - Effective from 1 January 2026
Owner: Finance Operations Team

1. SCOPE
This policy applies to all employees travelling on company business. All
figures are synthetic training data for the FinDesk application.

2. HOTEL ACCOMMODATION LIMITS
2.1 Domestic travel - metro cities (Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai,
    Kolkata, Hyderabad, Pune):
    Maximum hotel expense is Rs. 6,000 per night (room rate only).
2.2 Domestic travel - non-metro cities:
    Maximum hotel expense is Rs. 4,500 per night (room rate only).
2.3 International travel:
    Maximum hotel expense is USD 150 per night for most countries and
    USD 200 per night for high-cost cities (London, New York, Tokyo,
    Singapore, Zurich).
2.4 Taxes and service charges are reimbursed in addition to the room
    rate when printed on the hotel invoice.

3. DAILY PER DIEM
3.1 Meals and incidentals for domestic travel: Rs. 1,500 per day.
3.2 Meals and incidentals for international travel: USD 60 per day.
3.3 Per diem is paid automatically; receipts for meals are not required.

4. TRANSPORT
4.1 Economy class air travel is mandatory for flights under 6 hours.
4.2 Business class requires VP approval and flights over 8 hours.
4.3 Local transport: app-based cabs are preferred; auto and metro are
    reimbursed at actual cost with receipts.

5. BOOKING RULES
5.1 All bookings must be made through the approved travel portal.
5.2 Advance booking at least 7 days before departure saves cost and is
    encouraged; last-minute bookings need manager justification.
5.3 Changes and cancellations must be reported to Finance within 24 hours.

6. EXCEPTIONS
Any amount above the limits in section 2 requires written approval from
the Department Head before the booking is made. Retroactive approvals
are granted only in genuine emergencies.
