Department Budget Guidelines
Version 2.3 - Effective from 1 April 2026
Owner: Finance Operations Team

1. PURPOSE
These guidelines explain how department budgets are allocated, tracked and
reviewed. All budget figures used in training are synthetic data.

2. BUDGET ALLOCATION
2.1 Each department receives an annual budget split into four quarterly
    allocations at the start of the financial year.
2.2 Budget is allocated by cost centre; every PO and expense must carry
    a valid cost centre code.
2.3 Unspent quarterly amounts do not automatically carry forward; a
    carry-forward request must be approved by the Budget Controller.

3. UTILISATION FORMULA
3.1 Budget utilisation percentage = (Total spent / Total budget) x 100.
3.2 Remaining balance = Total budget - Total spent.
3.3 Forecast at completion = Total spent + committed but unpaid amounts.

4. MONITORING THRESHOLDS
4.1 Green: utilisation between 0% and 85% of the elapsed-time target.
4.2 Amber: utilisation exceeds the elapsed-time target by up to 10
    percentage points - the Department Head is notified.
4.3 Red: utilisation exceeds the elapsed-time target by more than 10
    percentage points - new commitments are frozen until reviewed.
4.4 A department that has spent more than 90% of its annual budget
    before the final quarter triggers a mandatory budget review.

5. VARIANCE ANALYSIS
5.1 Variance = Actual spend - Planned spend for the same period.
5.2 Variances beyond +/- 10% of plan must be explained in writing by
    the Department Head within 5 working days.
5.3 The Finance team publishes a variance report every quarter.

6. QUARTERLY REVIEW
6.1 Budget review meetings happen in the first week after each quarter ends.
6.2 Actions from the review (cuts, reallocations, freezes) are recorded
    and tracked to closure by the Budget Controller.
