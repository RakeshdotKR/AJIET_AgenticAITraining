Employee Expense Reimbursement Policy
Version 2.1 - Effective from 1 April 2026
Owner: Finance Operations Team

1. PURPOSE
This policy defines how employees claim reimbursement for legitimate business
expenses incurred while performing company duties. All amounts and records
referenced in this document are synthetic training data.

2. ELIGIBLE EXPENSES
2.1 The following expenses are eligible for reimbursement when supported by
    valid receipts and manager approval:
    - Domestic and international travel (air, rail, cab)
    - Hotel accommodation within policy limits
    - Client meeting meals (up to Rs. 1,500 per person per meal)
    - Office supplies and stationery purchased for business use
    - Professional conference fees with prior approval
    - Local transport for business purposes (auto, metro, cab)

2.2 The following expenses are NOT eligible:
    - Personal entertainment, alcohol and tobacco
    - Traffic fines and parking penalties
    - Expenses incurred after the employee's last working day
    - Family members' travel unless specifically approved in writing
    - Upgrade charges on personal convenience

3. CLAIM LIMITS AND THRESHOLDS
3.1 Receipts are mandatory for every expense of Rs. 500 or more.
3.2 Expenses below Rs. 500 may be claimed without receipts, but supporting
    notes (vendor name, date, purpose) are still required.
3.3 The maximum amount per single reimbursement claim is Rs. 75,000.
    Larger amounts must be split across multiple claims with justification.
3.4 Cash advances must be settled within 15 working days of return.

4. SUBMISSION DEADLINES
4.1 Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date.
4.2 Claims submitted between 31 and 60 days require Finance Head approval.
4.3 Claims older than 60 days are rejected automatically.

5. APPROVAL WORKFLOW
5.1 Employee submits claim with receipts attached.
5.2 Line manager reviews and approves within 3 working days.
5.3 Finance team verifies receipts and policy compliance within 5 days.
5.4 Approved amounts are credited in the next payroll cycle.

6. REVIEW
This policy is reviewed every six months by the Finance Operations Team.
