Procurement Policy
Version 2.0 - Effective from 1 March 2026
Owner: Finance Operations Team

1. SCOPE
This policy covers purchase of goods and services by all departments.
All thresholds below are synthetic training data.

2. PURCHASE ORDER REQUIREMENTS
2.1 A Purchase Order (PO) is mandatory for every purchase of Rs. 25,000
    or more. Purchases below Rs. 25,000 may use a petty-cash voucher.
2.2 POs must state: item description, quantity, unit price, total amount,
    GSTIN of the vendor, delivery date and cost centre code.
2.3 Retrospective POs (goods received before the PO was issued) are
    allowed only once per vendor per quarter with Finance Head approval.

3. COMPETITIVE QUOTES
3.1 Purchases from Rs. 25,000 to Rs. 2,00,000 require at least two
    written quotations.
3.2 Purchases above Rs. 2,00,000 require at least three written
    quotations and a comparative statement.
3.3 Single-source purchase is allowed only when the vendor is on the
    approved proprietary list or the item is a genuine sole-source item,
    with written justification from the Department Head.

4. APPROVED VENDOR LIST
4.1 Vendors must be on the Approved Vendor List (AVL) before a PO is
    issued. New vendors require a due-diligence check by Procurement.
4.2 Vendor performance is reviewed twice a year: delivery timeliness,
    quality and invoice accuracy.

5. RECEIPT AND ACCEPTANCE
5.1 Goods must be inspected within 3 working days of delivery.
5.2 A Goods Receipt Note (GRN) is raised after inspection; the GRN is
    required for the three-way match before payment.
5.3 Service purchases require a signed acceptance note from the
    requesting manager.

6. DEPARTMENT BUDGET LINKAGE
6.1 Every PO must carry a valid cost centre and budget code.
6.2 If the department's budget utilisation is already above 90%, new POs
    need additional approval from the Budget Controller.
