Invoice Processing Guidelines
Version 1.2 - Effective from 1 April 2026
Owner: Finance Operations Team

1. PURPOSE
These guidelines describe how the Accounts Payable team processes vendor
invoices. All invoices referenced here are synthetic training data.

2. REQUIRED INVOICE FIELDS
A vendor invoice is valid only if ALL of the following fields are present:
2.1 Unique invoice number (alphanumeric, max 20 characters)
2.2 Invoice date in DD-MM-YYYY format
2.3 Vendor name and vendor GSTIN (15-character GST identification number)
2.4 Purchase Order (PO) reference number for purchases above Rs. 25,000
2.5 Description of goods or services with quantity and unit price
2.6 Taxable value, GST breakdown (CGST, SGST or IGST) and total amount
2.7 Vendor bank account reference matching the vendor master

3. PROCESSING WORKFLOW
3.1 Invoice received -> registered in the invoice log with a receipt ID.
3.2 Field completeness check against section 2 (automated where possible).
3.3 Three-way match: PO vs GRN vs invoice (see Vendor Payment Policy 3.1).
3.4 Discrepancy check: price variance, quantity variance, tax calculation.
3.5 Manager approval for the cost centre, then payment scheduling.

4. DISCREPANCY HANDLING
4.1 Missing PO reference: hold the invoice, request PO from Procurement.
4.2 Price variance above 2% of PO price: hold for buyer confirmation.
4.3 Missing GSTIN: return to vendor; input tax credit cannot be claimed.
4.4 Duplicate invoice number: reject immediately and notify the vendor.
4.5 Arithmetic error in totals: recalculate and request a corrected copy.

5. TIMELINES
5.1 Invoices with no discrepancies are scheduled within 7 working days.
5.2 Invoices on hold are reviewed weekly; unresolved holds older than
    30 days are escalated to the Finance Head.
5.3 All invoice statuses must be updated in the log on the same day.

6. RECORD KEEPING
Invoices and supporting documents are retained for 8 years as required
by statutory norms.
