Vendor Payment Policy
Version 1.4 - Effective from 1 February 2026
Owner: Finance Operations Team

1. PURPOSE
This policy governs how the company pays its vendors and suppliers. All
vendor records and amounts in this document are synthetic training data.

2. PAYMENT TERMS
2.1 Standard payment term is Net-30: payment is due 30 days after a valid
    invoice is received and accepted.
2.2 Vendors on strategic contracts may have Net-45 or Net-60 terms as
    written in the signed contract.
2.3 Early payment discount: 2% discount is available if payment is made
    within 10 days of invoice receipt (2/10 Net-30).
2.4 Payments are released only on Fridays, unless a critical payment is
    approved by the Finance Head.

3. THREE-WAY MATCH REQUIREMENT
3.1 Every invoice must match three documents before payment:
    - Purchase Order (PO) issued by Procurement
    - Goods Receipt Note (GRN) or service acceptance note
    - The vendor invoice itself
3.2 Quantity and unit price on the invoice must not exceed the PO by more
    than 2%. Anything above 2% is held for review.
3.3 Invoices without a PO reference are held and returned to the vendor
    for correction, except for invoices below Rs. 25,000.

4. VENDOR BANK DETAILS
4.1 Bank account changes must be verified by calling the vendor on a
    previously known phone number before the next payment run.
4.2 Payment is made only to the bank account registered in the vendor
    master; partial payments to third-party accounts are prohibited.

5. DISPUTES AND HOLDS
5.1 Disputed invoices are placed on hold with a reason code.
5.2 The vendor is notified within 2 working days of the hold.
5.3 Held invoices are re-evaluated every week by the Accounts Payable team.

6. LATE PAYMENT
6.1 If payment is delayed beyond the agreed term, Finance must record the
    reason in the payment log.
6.2 Late payment charges above Rs. 10,000 require Finance Head approval.
